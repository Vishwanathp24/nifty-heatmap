Scanners — F&O stocks, self-tracked

For technical research, not a trading recommendation.

Opening Range Breakout

Range forms in the first N minutes after 09:15 IST; a later break above/below it is flagged here.

Buy / Sell (Bullish / Bearish)

Daily and intraday, each: Close vs SMA(20) · Close vs each of the last 5 bars' High/Low · RSI(14) vs 60 (buy) / 40 (sell). Daily uses real NSE EOD history (ready now); intraday is self-tracked and persisted across days. A stock only "qualifies" once both timeframes pass.

15-Min Breakout

Pure 15-min, independent of the scanner above: Close breaks above/below the prior bar's rolling 20-period Close high, plus Volume vs its own 20-period SMA. The Sell version deliberately wants volume below average (not a spike) — that's the source rule, not a mistake.

Downtrend Scanner

Daily: Close < EMA20 < EMA50 < EMA200, RSI(14) < 45, ADX(14) > 20 — real NSE daily candles. A dedicated bearish-trend screen, not a Buy/Sell Scanner variant.

Bullish Intraday Scanner

Daily and 1-hour, each: Close vs SMA(20) · Close vs each of the last 5 bars' High · RSI(14) > 60 - the exact rule from vp-bullish-intraday-stock. Lists every stock passing the 7 daily conditions; once 1-hour history is ready (can take a few trading days to build up), the Status column shows which of those also pass all 7 intraday conditions ("✓ Qualified") vs daily-only - so a listed stock isn't necessarily a full 14-condition match yet.

Bearish Intraday Scanner

Daily and 1-hour, each: Close vs SMA(20) · Close vs each of the last 5 bars' Low · RSI(14) < 40 - the exact rule from vp-bearish-intraday-scanner. Lists every stock passing the 7 daily conditions; once 1-hour history is ready (can take a few trading days to build up), the Status column shows which of those also pass all 7 intraday conditions ("✓ Qualified") vs daily-only - so a listed stock isn't necessarily a full 14-condition match yet.

Bullish Morning Scanner

ALL 16 required: today's range (High−Low) beats each of the last 7 days' own range · green candle (Close > Open, Close > yesterday's Close) · this week's and this month's Close above their own Open · yesterday's volume > 10,000 · SMA(20) > SMA(40) > SMA(60) of Close · today's volume > 1.25× yesterday's · latest 15-min candle's Close > its own Open. "ROCE < 30" and "EPS > 0" from the original spec are not evaluated - this app has no fundamentals data source. Lists every stock passing the 15 daily/weekly/monthly conditions; once 15-min history is ready, the Status column shows which also pass the 16th ("✓ All 16") vs 15-of-16 only.

BTST Scanner

Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow - daily only, ALL 5 required: today's volume > 3× its own 5-day average · RSI(14) > 65 · today's Open and Close both above yesterday's · today's Close within 15% of its own 250-day high. The exact rule from vp-buy-today-sell-tomorrow-btst, which runs on the cash segment (~2000 NSE stocks); scoped to the F&O universe here instead, so it will only surface F&O-eligible names, not genuine small/micro-caps the real scan can also show. No intraday leg, so this is ready immediately.
